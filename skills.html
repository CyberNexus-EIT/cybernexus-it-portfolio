05 / Workflow

ADSTOM Development Lifecycle

Development follows ADSTOM: Analyze, Develop, Secure, Test, Operate, and Maintain.

01

Analyze

Define requirements, users, scope, architecture, resources, risks, and technical direction.

02

Develop

Build software, infrastructure, automation, databases, interfaces, and supporting services.

03

Secure

Apply secure design, coding practices, access controls, dependency checks, and secret protection.

04

Test

Verify functionality, compatibility, reliability, performance, and security.

05

Operate

Deploy, configure, connect, monitor, and operate systems in their intended environments.

06

Maintain

Monitor systems, resolve issues, update components, improve reliability, and continuously evolve projects.
