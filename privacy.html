Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, protected, retained, and disclosed when you interact with the CyberNexus IT Portfolio.

02 / Information

Information We May Collect

Information may be collected when reasonably necessary to provide available features, respond to communications, operate the project, maintain security, or meet applicable obligations.

Submitted Information

Information You Provide

This may include your name, email address, messages, account information, project information, or other information that you voluntarily submit through available forms or communication channels.

Account Information

Account and Access Data

If account features are enabled, the project may process information required to create, authenticate, maintain, secure, and manage an account.

Technical Information

System and Diagnostic Data

Depending on the deployment, technical data may include browser, device, connection, access, diagnostic, logging, and security information generated during normal operation.

03 / Use

How Information May Be Used

Information may be processed for communication, project operation, maintenance, security, and other legitimate purposes connected with the CyberNexus IT Portfolio.

01

Communication

Respond to messages, inquiries, professional requests, project discussions, collaboration requests, and other communications initiated through available channels.

02

Operation

Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, improve, administer, and secure the project and its available features.

03

Security

Detect, investigate, prevent, and respond to unauthorized access, abuse, fraud, technical problems, and security incidents.

04 / Sharing

Information Sharing

Personal information should not be sold or disclosed unnecessarily. Any disclosure depends on the actual deployment, enabled services, applicable law, and legitimate operational needs.

Service Providers

Operational Services

Where necessary for operation, information may be processed by hosting, infrastructure, communication, security, analytics, or other service providers configured for the project. Access should be limited to the services required for their intended function.

Legal and Security

Required Disclosure

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect users, protect the project, or investigate suspected security incidents or unlawful activity.

05 / Security

Information Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or loss.

Depending on the deployment and enabled features, safeguards may include authentication, access controls, secure communications, protected credentials, input validation, logging, backups, monitoring, dependency management, security testing, and controlled administrative access.

Security is an ongoing process. Controls should therefore be reviewed, tested, maintained, and improved as the project, infrastructure, and potential threats change.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting sensitive information that is not necessary for the intended purpose of the project.

06 / Retention

Information Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose, legitimate operational needs, security requirements, or applicable legal obligations.

Purpose-Based

Limited Retention

Information may be removed, anonymized, or securely disposed of when it is no longer reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected.

Operational Records

Security and Legal Needs

Certain records may need to be retained for security investigations, dispute resolution, system integrity, legal obligations, or other legitimate operational requirements.

07 / Rights

Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information processed through the project.

01

Access

Request information about personal data associated with your interaction with the project, where applicable.

02

Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information where applicable.

03

Deletion

Where legally applicable, request deletion of personal information that is no longer required.

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, additional rights may apply, including rights concerning restriction, objection, portability, consent withdrawal, or complaints to an appropriate privacy or regulatory authority.

Requests may require reasonable verification before action is taken to help protect the account and information involved.

08 / Changes

Privacy Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the project, its features, services, infrastructure, or applicable requirements change.

Updated versions should be published through this page. The version displayed on the website is intended to describe the privacy practices applicable to the current deployment.

Users should periodically review this page for material changes to how information may be collected, used, protected, retained, or disclosed.

09 / Contact

Privacy Questions

For privacy-related questions, requests, corrections, or other concerns, use the available contact channel.
