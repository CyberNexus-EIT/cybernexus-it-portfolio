Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the CyberNexus IT Portfolio Platform may collect, use, protect, retain, and handle information when you interact with the platform.

02 / Information

Information We May Collect

The platform is designed to collect and process information only when reasonably necessary for available functions, communication, operation, and security.

Provided Information

Information You Submit

This may include your name, email address, messages, account information, project information, or other information that you voluntarily provide through the platform.

Account Information

Account and Access Data

If account functionality is enabled, the platform may process information needed to create, authenticate, manage, secure, and maintain an account.

Technical Information

System and Diagnostic Data

Depending on deployment configuration, technical information may include browser, device, connection, access, diagnostic, logging, and security-related information.

03 / Use

How Information May Be Used

Information may be processed for legitimate platform, communication, operational, and security purposes.

01

Communication

Respond to messages, inquiries, professional requests, project discussions, and other communications initiated through the platform.

02

Operation

Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, improve, and administer the platform and its available features.

03

Security

Detect, investigate, prevent, and respond to unauthorized access, abuse, fraud, technical problems, and security incidents.

04 / Sharing

Information Sharing

Personal information should not be sold or disclosed unnecessarily. Any sharing depends on the platform's actual deployment and enabled services.

Service Providers

Operational Services

Where required for operation, information may be processed by hosting, infrastructure, communication, security, analytics, or other service providers configured for the platform. Processing should be limited to the purpose for which the service is provided.

Legal and Security

Required Disclosure

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect users, protect the platform, or investigate suspected security incidents or unlawful activity.

05 / Security

Data Protection

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be applied to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

Depending on deployment and enabled features, safeguards may include authentication, access controls, secure communications, protected credentials, input validation, logging, backups, monitoring, dependency management, security testing, and controlled administrative access.

Security is an ongoing process. Controls should therefore be reviewed, tested, maintained, and improved as the platform, infrastructure, and potential threats change.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting sensitive information that is not necessary for the intended purpose of the platform.

06 / Retention

Information Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose, legitimate operational needs, security requirements, or applicable legal obligations.

Purpose-Based

Limited Retention

Information may be removed, anonymized, or securely disposed of when it is no longer reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected.

Operational Records

Security and Legal Needs

Certain records may need to be retained for security investigations, dispute resolution, system integrity, legal obligations, or other legitimate operational requirements.

07 / Rights

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information processed through the platform.

01

Access

Request information about personal data associated with your interaction with the platform.

02

Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information where applicable.

03

Deletion

Where legally applicable, request deletion of personal information that is no longer required.

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, additional rights may apply, including rights concerning restriction, objection, portability, consent withdrawal, or complaints to an appropriate privacy or regulatory authority.

Requests may require reasonable verification before action is taken in order to protect the account and information involved.

08 / Changes

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the platform, its features, services, infrastructure, or applicable requirements change.

Updated versions should be published through this page. The version displayed on the platform is intended to describe the privacy practices applicable to the current deployment.

Users should periodically review this page for material changes to how information may be collected, used, protected, retained, or disclosed.

09 / Contact

Privacy Questions

For privacy-related questions, requests, corrections, or other concerns, use the platform's contact channel.
