ADSTOM

Development Methodology

CyberNexus projects follow ADSTOM: Analyze, Develop, Secure, Test, Operate, and Maintain.

01

Analyze

Define requirements, users, scope, architecture, resources, constraints, and technical direction.

02

Develop

Build applications, frontend, backend, databases, infrastructure, automation, and supporting components.

03

Secure

Apply secure design, secure coding, access controls, dependency checks, secret protection, and security controls.

04

Test

Verify functionality, quality, compatibility, reliability, performance, and security.

05

Operate

Configure, deploy, connect, monitor, and operate systems in their intended environments.

06

Maintain

Monitor systems, resolve issues, update components, improve reliability, and continuously evolve projects.
